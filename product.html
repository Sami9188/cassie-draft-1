The scent

Wear it well

Three habits for a longer trail.

  1. 01

    Pulse points

    Spray from about 15 cm onto the wrists, neck and inner elbows, where warmth carries the scent.

  2. 02

    Moisturised skin

    Apply after a shower on unscented, moisturised skin. Fragrance holds longer on skin that isn't dry.

  3. 03

    Cool and dark

    Keep the bottle in its box, away from heat and sunlight, so the top notes stay bright.
