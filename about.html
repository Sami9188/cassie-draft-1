About CASSIE

A perfume house that opened its doors to brands.

CASSIE began as an atelier creating its own fragrances in Dubai, sourcing the finest ingredients from across the globe. The same perfumers, suppliers and production team now build fragrance brands for partners around the world.

Our story

We sell our own perfumes too, so we know the shelf.

Because CASSIE makes and sells its own collection, we know what a product needs to do on the shelf, not just in the lab. That experience goes into every brief we take on: the scent has to last, the bottle has to feel right in the hand, and the carton has to arrive ready for the market it was made for.

Today CASSIE works with brand owners, importers and retail chains who want fine fragrance made properly, with one partner responsible for the liquid, the glass and the box.

How we work

Four things every partner can expect.

  1. 01

    The batch you approve is the batch you get

    Every formula is recorded, every batch coded and a retained sample kept, so your reorder smells exactly like your launch.

  2. 02

    You see the process

    Briefs, sample rounds, timelines and documents are shared with you as the work moves, not hidden behind a final invoice.

  3. 03

    Your brand leads

    We are the name behind your product, not on it. Client work stays confidential, with an NDA on request.

  4. 04

    Plain answers

    Clear MOQs, clear lead times and clear pricing in your quotation, from an account manager who replies within one business day.
