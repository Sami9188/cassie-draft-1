Before you ask

Common questions.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQs depend on the bottle and packaging you choose. As a guide, private label starts from about 1,000 units to confirm. Your quotation states the exact figure.

Can I smell samples before I order?

Yes. Request a sample kit of white-label fragrances, glass and cap samples, either here or by ticking the sample box in the quotation form.

How long does a project take?

It depends on development rounds and packaging lead times. Your account manager confirms a timeline with the quotation typical range to confirm.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Your brief stays confidential and we sign an NDA on request before you share formulas or artwork.

Do you ship outside the UAE?

Yes. We pack, document and ship finished goods and concentrated oils for importers and distributors, with support on export paperwork.
