How a CASSIE product is made

From brief to dispatch in six controlled steps.

  1. 01

    Brief & Consultation

    Target market, price point, fragrance direction, quantities and launch date agreed with your account manager.

  2. 02

    Formulation

    Perfumers build or match the accord. Samples are submitted for your approval, with revisions until sign-off.

  3. 03

    Compounding & Maceration

    Approved concentrate is blended with perfumer's alcohol and rested under controlled conditions so the scent matures.

  4. 04

    Chilling & Filtration

    The juice is chilled and filtered for clarity and stability before it reaches the filling line.

  5. 05

    Filling, Crimping & Assembly

    Bottles are filled, crimped or screwed, capped, labelled and boxed to your packaging specification.

  6. 06

    Quality Control & Dispatch

    Every batch is checked, coded for traceability and packed with its documents for local or export shipment.
