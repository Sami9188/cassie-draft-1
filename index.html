How a CASSIE product is made

From brief to dispatch
in six controlled steps.

  1. 01

    Brief & Consultation

    Target market, price point, fragrance direction, quantities and launch date agreed with your account manager.

  2. 02

    Formulation

    Perfumers build or match the accord. Samples are submitted for your approval, with revisions until sign-off.

  3. 03

    Compounding & Maceration

    Approved concentrate is blended with perfumer's alcohol and rested under controlled conditions so the scent matures.

  4. 04

    Chilling & Filtration

    The juice is chilled and filtered for clarity and stability before it reaches the filling line.

  5. 05

    Filling, Crimping & Assembly

    Bottles are filled, crimped or screwed, capped, labelled and boxed to your packaging specification.

  6. 06

    Quality Control & Dispatch

    Every batch is checked, coded for traceability and packed with its documents for local or export shipment.

Inside the facility

Capability you can audit.

Buyers ask the same questions before they commit. Here are the answers, written for procurement teams.

Compounding & maceration capacity

Stainless-steel compounding vessels and dedicated maceration storage, scheduled by batch so your production never waits on someone else's. Capacity figures to be confirmed by CASSIE.

Automated filling & packaging lines

Semi-automatic and automatic filling, crimping, capping, labelling and cellophane wrapping for 20ml to 200ml formats, including travel sprays and gift sets.

Raw material & component sourcing

Established supply of fragrance concentrates, perfumer's alcohol grades, luxury glass, zamac caps, pumps and rigid boxes, so you deal with one partner instead of five suppliers.

Decoration & branding in-house

Screen printing, hot-foil stamping, labels, embossed cartons and custom inserts coordinated through CASSIE's packaging team.

Minimum order quantities

Flexible MOQs designed for new brands as well as large retail programmes. Exact MOQs depend on bottle and packaging choices and are confirmed in your quotation.
