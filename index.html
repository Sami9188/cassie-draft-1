Process

Six steps. No surprises.

  1. Step 1Brief

    Who wears it, where it sells, price point, target market.

  2. Step 2Develop

    Perfumer builds options; you evaluate sample rounds.

  3. Step 3Test

    Stability, compatibility with bottle and pump, IFRA check.

  4. Step 4Pack

    Bottle, cap, pump, label and carton sourced or supplied.

  5. Step 5Fill

    Maceration, filtration, filling, coding and QC per batch.

  6. Step 6Document & ship

    Compliance file for the destination market, then dispatch.
